Miso ramen with a soft egg

  • Time: 25 min
  • Serves 2
  • Easy

A quick bowl from the cupboard: miso broth, springy noodles and a jammy egg.

Ingredients

  • 2 nests of ramen noodles
  • 1 litre chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 spring onions
  • A handful of spinach

Steps

  1. Boil the eggs for 6½ minutes, then cool them in cold water and peel.

  2. Warm the stock and whisk in the miso. Do not let it boil.

  3. Cook the noodles for 3 minutes and drain.

  4. Wilt the spinach in the broth, then pour over the noodles and top with halved eggs and spring onion.
